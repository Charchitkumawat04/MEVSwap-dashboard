## ⚡ What is MEVSwap?

[View Dashboard on Dune](https://dune.com/charchitku/how-mevswap-looks)

**MEVSwap** is the **first decentralized exchange (DEX)** built with **native protection against MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)** — developed by Merkle.

This innovative DEX introduces several standout features:

- 🛡️ **Sandwich Attack Protection:** Every trade on MEVSwap is automatically shielded from sandwich attacks, regardless of the wallet or interface used.
- 💰 **Arbitrage Revenue for LPs:** MEVSwap captures arbitrage profits through **MEV Bids** and redistributes them to liquidity providers (LPs), significantly enhancing APYs.
- 🔄 **Improved Swap Prices:** Retail traders are prioritized over arbitrageurs, helping reduce slippage and deliver better prices on one side of the trade.
- 🔧 **Built on Uniswap V4 Hooks:** MEVSwap leverages Uniswap V4, modifying only the "before" and "after" swap hooks. Liquidity management remains untouched — ensuring LPs have full freedom to deposit or withdraw anytime.

MEVSwap represents a next-gen DEX experience, aligning incentives for both LPs and traders while offering robust MEV protection by design.
